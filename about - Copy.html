Our story

Beauty in Every Line

Mystric Mehandhi is a Kandy-based henna studio creating personal bridal art, celebration designs, natural henna products, and practical classes.

The artist

Meet Rahma

Rahma is the artist behind Mystric Mehandhi. Her work is built around clean detail, natural paste, and a calm appointment experience from the first message to the final aftercare note.

Every design is shaped around the client, the celebration, the outfit, and the time available - never copied from a fixed template.

Our Services

From a full bridal story to a simple festive trail, every service begins with your style and ends with clear aftercare guidance.

Bridal Mehndi

Custom full-hand and leg designs, bridal trials, initials, motifs, and family coordination.

Party & Simple Mehndi

The Mystric ritual

Our Process

From fresh ingredients to a beautiful stain, each detail is prepared with intention.

01

Choose Pure Ingredients

We begin with natural henna and stain-safe ingredients chosen for a rich, dependable result.

02

Prepare Fresh Cones

Paste is rested, checked, and filled into clean cones for smooth, controlled lines.

03

Apply With Care

Your design is created at an unhurried pace, with thoughtful placement and clean finishing.

04

Guide the Stain

You leave with clear aftercare advice and products that help your henna deepen beautifully.
